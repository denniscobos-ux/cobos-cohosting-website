About Us

A Superhost duo fueled by a passion for managing rental properties and creating exceptional experiences for owners and guests alike.

◆ Our Story

Dynamic Backgrounds, Unified Mission

Cobos Cohosting was born from a shared passion for real estate and hospitality. We come from different but complementary personal and professional backgrounds and leverage our skills and experiences to deliver a comprehensive and high-quality property management service.

Our mission is simple: to give property owners a truly hands-off experience while maximizing the potential of their investment. We treat every property as if it were our own, because we know what it takes — we are property owners ourselves.

◆ Meet the Team

The People Behind Cobos Cohosting

Together, we bring over a decade of hospitality and technology expertise to managing your property.

DC

Dennis Cobos

Co-Founder, Technology & Operations

A Superhost managing his vacation rental in Panama City Beach, FL. Dennis has developed a passion for the rental space and for providing 5-star guest experiences. Born in Guatemala, he speaks Spanish fluently which helps him communicate and connect with Spanish-speaking guests easily and effectively.

Leveraging his 10 years of experience in the technology space, Dennis brings a system-based mindset where he prioritizes operational efficiencies, automation, and streamlined processes. He focuses on finding solutions to problems and truly enjoys the technical and process side of hosting rental properties.

PB

Patricia Berman

Co-Founder, Guest Experience & Communications

With over two decades of experience, Patricia has impeccably supported top-level executives in the finance sector, skillfully managing calendars, orchestrating events, and delivering white-glove service at renowned financial institutions worldwide.

As a seasoned real estate investor with properties spanning multiple states, Patricia proudly holds a stellar 4.98-star rating. She approaches hosting with an unwavering passion, steadfast integrity, and a genuine care for every individual she encounters. When it comes to entrusting your home, there is no one more qualified or trustworthy.
