◆ The Sweet Spot

Why mid-term rentals?

Mid-term rentals — furnished stays of 30 days or more — sit in the sweet spot between long-term leases and short-term vacation rentals.

📈

Higher Income

You get higher income than a standard lease, typically 20–40% more per month.

🔄

Less Turnover

Far less turnover and wear than nightly Airbnb stays. Longer stays mean less cleaning, fewer transitions, and lower costs.

👥

Reliable Tenants

Traveling nurses, relocating families, and remote professionals who treat your home with care.
